Short notes for durable ideas.

The blog keeps the project story, commands, screenshots, and context. Notes keep one reusable idea at a time, so they can be linked from multiple posts later.

Most notes sit somewhere in a larger loop:

**Intent → Representation → Execution → Feedback**

Ideas begin as product questions, business goals, engineering problems, or personal observations. 

They become representations such as requirements, notes, models, telemetry, and documentation. They are then tested through software, agents, infrastructure, manufacturing, or physical experiments. 

The result feeds back into the next decision.

The recurring areas are:

- Product, business, services, sales, and distribution
- Agents, AI-assisted work, and operating systems
- Knowledge management, documentation, and accountability
- Self-hosting, infrastructure, security, and data systems
- IoT, telemetry, AIoT, and control loops
- Engineering, physics, MBSD, mechanisms, and NVH
- CAD, 3D printing, manufacturing, and mechatronics
- Travel, maps, and field automation

The categories are connected rather than exclusive. 

The useful question is usually: **what intent is being represented, how can it be tested, and what did the result teach?**
