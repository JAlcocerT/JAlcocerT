date: 2025-08-02
tags: ["formbricks", "lead-generation", "forms", "waiting-list"]
description: "Formbricks is useful when a static page needs to collect qualified demand without growing into a custom backend."
---

Formbricks is a fast bridge between a static page and real market feedback.

Instead of building a backend first, a waiting list or landing page can route clicks into a Formbricks survey, prefill context with a survey URL and question ID, and send responses to Google Sheets or webhooks. That keeps the page simple while still capturing more than an email.

The useful move is to ask questions that qualify intent: who the visitor is, what problem they have, and what they expect next. The form should turn anonymous interest into a lead worth following up.

For early validation, Formbricks is a product sensor, not just a contact form.
