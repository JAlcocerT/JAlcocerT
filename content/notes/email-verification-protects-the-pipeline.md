---

An email in a form is not yet a usable lead.

Before enriching, importing, or sequencing contacts, verify that the address exists and can receive mail. 

That can happen through managed tools such as MillionVerifier or NeverBounce, OSS checks such as KnockMail, or product flows such as PocketBase plus Mailtrap verification links.

Verification is not just hygiene.

It protects deliverability, saves API and campaign budget, and keeps the sales pipeline from filling with fake intent.

Automated outreach should start with valid contact data, not hope.
