date: 2024-09-10
tags: ["email", "newsletter", "marketing", "forms"]
description: "Newsletter platforms are useful when they own subscriptions, forms, automations, and unsubscribe state."
---

A newsletter tool is not just a way to send messages.

Its real job is audience state: forms, subscriber records, groups, automations, webhooks, preferences, and unsubscribes. 

MailerLite works well for simple website subscriptions because a static site can embed the form and let the platform handle the list. 

Listmonk gives a similar ownership model when you want the stack closer to your own infrastructure.

For a landing page, this keeps frontend code small. For a business, it keeps consent and subscription state in one place.

The list is the asset, not the popup.
